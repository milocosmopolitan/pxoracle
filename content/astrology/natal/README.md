# Original English natal content, natal.en.1

The `authored/` files are the editable source. `python3 scripts/build-natal-content.py`
produces the three public packs and SHA-256 manifest; `--check` verifies exact
reproduction. Sentences are external JSON, never embedded in Rust/WASM. No scraped
interpretation library, LLM runtime or paid API is used. New wording is part of this
repository's original content; it does not inherit GeoNames' license. The atlas has
its own attribution and retained source under `../atlas/`.

  [FAQ](https://cafeastrology.com/freereportsfaq.html): placement/pair discussion,
  summary-to-detail navigation and unknown-time disclosure informed the scope.
  Their sentences were not copied, translated or closely paraphrased. Their Placidus
  house output is not the numerical reference for this Whole Sign implementation.
- [NASA, inner planets and elongation](https://science.nasa.gov/learn/basics-of-space-flight/chapter1-2/)
  and [Hong Kong Observatory, apparent motion](https://my.weather.gov.hk/en/education/astronomy-and-time/astronomy/00405-apparent-motion-of-planets-explained.html):
  the approximately 28° Mercury and 48° Venus maximum elongations constrain the

- Numerical qualifications remain in
  [astronomy-reference.md](../../../docs/validation/astronomy-reference.md).
  Symbolic interpretation is not astronomical evidence of personality or prediction.

There are 120 body/sign articles, 120 body/house articles and 12 Ascendant articles.
Each has summary, body, constructive practice, tension and reflection question.
Sign passages separate personal, social and generational scope; supported houses
provide a personal context without claiming that an outer planet's sign is unique
to one person. Pair articles combine an original pair-specific discussion with an
aspect-specific treatment/tension. Practical prompts are shared within a pair where
they address its common relationship, not generated by substituting planet names.

## Reachable major-aspect coverage

The calculation engine accepts conjunction/sextile/square/trine/opposition orbs
from 0° through 15°, inclusive. Default limits are 8/4/6/6/8°.
45 unordered pairs × 5 types is 225, before physical constraints:

- Sun–Mercury supports conjunction only: even a 15° sextile orb starts at 45°.
- Sun–Venus supports conjunction and, with a wide orb, sextile. Square starts at
  75°, outside its elongation. Trine and opposition are also excluded.
- Mercury–Venus excludes trine/opposition. The sum of the conservative elongation
  limits is about 76°, below the 105° minimum of a 15° trine. Square remains in the
  conservative supported set; absence from sampling is not proof of impossibility.

This excludes nine combinations and retains 216. `aspect-witnesses.json` records
214 actual emitted keys with date/orb evidence from daily 12:00 UTC calculations
across 1900–2100 at maximum configured orbs. Reproduce with
`cargo run --release --locked -p pxoracle-astrology --example aspect-coverage`.
The adapter integration test recalculates every witness and resolves its pair-specific
article. A daily grid is a coverage witness, not a proof that nothing happens between
samples. No native/WASM agreement is presented as independent accuracy evidence.

Two conservative keys have explicit **pair-level fallback**, visible in the report:
`aspect.mercury.venus.square` and `aspect.neptune.pluto.opposition`. They have no
daily witness in the supported range. Their original text discusses the pair without
pretending to provide a verified aspect-specific personal claim. All 216 keys resolve;
214 have witnessed, specific treatments. Future finer coverage can replace a fallback
only under new semantics/content versions.

## Deterministic selection

External weights: Sun/Moon 100; Mercury/Venus/Mars 80; Jupiter/Saturn 50;
Uranus/Neptune/Pluto 25. Sign priority uses that weight; house priority adds 10;
Ascendant priority is 110. These are editorial choices, not quality/personality scores.

Aspect priority is the sum of the two body weights, type weight (conjunction 40,
opposition 35, square 30, trine 20, sextile 15), and rounded normalized closeness
`100 × (1 − orb/configuredLimit)`. Exact zero-limit aspects receive closeness 100.
Higher priority comes first; ties use ascending stable reference ID. The first five
supported aspects become the overview relationships. Pair order is the engine's
canonical body order; reversing the list of aspects does not change the plan.

Sixteen external repetition/complement/tension rules choose evidence from **distinct
bodies**. A sign and house of the same body cannot prove repetition alone. For each
rule, the highest combined placement priority wins; ties use the two placement IDs.
Rule priority is added, all matches are sorted by priority then rule ID, and the first
three are shown. Competing needs are described as a tension to negotiate, not mutually
exclusive labels. No strong match uses the explicit weak-pattern passage. No themes
are asserted from interval samples.

Each of four life topics selects up to three relevant items using the same stable
priority, suppressing duplicate single-body or pair keys. Full placements and aspects
remain available regardless of overview rank. Theme paragraphs are deliberately
reusable editorial relationships; their linked chart evidence changes with the chart.
The system does not claim to have authored a novel personal narrative.

Evidence orbs are rounded to **0.000001°** for portable JSON plans. Calculation
longitudes, speeds and aspect inclusion keep full f64 precision. Plan parity is exact;
native/WASM numerical chart comparisons use the established 1e-8° tolerance.

## Editorial review

See [the representative review matrix](../../../docs/validation/natal/editorial-review.md)
and the full HTML example. Agent review checked evidence, narrative tensions and
practical prompts. Automated coverage and agent review are **not independent human
