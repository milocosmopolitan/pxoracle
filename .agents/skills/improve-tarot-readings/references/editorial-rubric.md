# Editorial rubric

Assess complete compositions, not isolated good sentences. Record pass/concern/fail with observable evidence; do not replace judgment with lexical novelty.

| Dimension | Review question |
| --- | --- |
| Grounding | Does the explanation name or clearly connect the actual card meaning, orientation, position and participating relationship? |
| Synthesis | Does it explain how at least two evidenced cards interact rather than stringing together three meanings? |
| Topic | Does work address scope/resources/feedback, relationships reciprocity/boundaries, creativity drafts/constraints, growth habits/reflection where the cards support it? |
| Intent | Does present clarify conditions, crossroads compare values/unknowns/checks, and action offer a bounded experiment? |
| Coherence | Do overall, connection, position sections, action and question support one another without incompatible advice? |
| Specificity | Can the user perform the action or answer the question without inventing the missing task? |
| Reversal | Is blockage, excess, inward focus, review or recovery grounded in the authored lens rather than mechanical negativity? |
| Fallback | Is the absence of a supported shared relationship disclosed without an invented story? |
| Voice | Is the English natural, concise and non-repetitive across adjacent sections? |
| Agency | Does it avoid guaranteed outcomes, mind-reading, diagnosis, demographic stereotypes and coercive instructions? |

Inspect Death, Tower and Devil for unsupported doom; court cards for stereotypes; relationships for claims about another person's hidden feelings. Keep high-stakes questions reflective. Do not insert blanket warning paragraphs into every reading.

Accept shared prose when the underlying advice is legitimately the same. Reject prefix-only personalization when it leaves the user's intent unanswered. Do not force different text for every combination or increase synonym variation just to reduce equality counts. Explain why each changed clause fits its context and evidence.

Use sensitive-card, upright/reversed/mixed, repetition/tension/complement, primary/secondary relation, position permutation, fallback and control cases. Representative coverage is purposeful, not a random estimate of population rates.
