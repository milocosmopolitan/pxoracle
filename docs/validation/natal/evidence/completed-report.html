Terms and limits

Tropical

Signs are twelve equal 30-degree sectors beginning at the March equinox, not the unequal modern astronomical constellations.

Whole-Sign

The entire sign containing the Ascendant is house one; each following sign is the next house. This report does not use Placidus.

Longitude

Geocentric ecliptic longitude is an angle around the zodiac. The crystal is a symbolic angle map, not a scale model of planetary orbits.

Ascendant

The Ascendant is the intersection of the ecliptic with the eastern horizon at the calculated time and place. It is omitted when time or latitude is unsupported.

Orb

Orb is the angular distance from an exact aspect. Smaller orbs receive more editorial emphasis; this is not a scientific strength or personality score.

Conjunction

Near 0 degrees: the paired functions occupy closely related territory. Combining them can help or complicate how each is expressed.

Sextile

Near 60 degrees: an available cooperation that may benefit from deliberate use. The actual pair gives the relationship its specific subject.

Square

Near 90 degrees: functions may interrupt or challenge one another. The interpretation asks for a workable adjustment, not a prediction of harm.

Trine

Near 120 degrees: a compatible pattern can be readily available. Ease does not guarantee that it will be used thoughtfully.

Opposition

Near 180 degrees: contrasting needs may seem located at opposite poles. A conscious agreement or rhythm can give each a place.

Retrograde

Negative signed longitudinal speed means apparent retrograde motion from Earth. The table reports it; this release does not add a separate retrograde personality interpretation.

Elements

Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius), Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces). Counts describe supported placements, not measured character or a deficit.

Modalities

Cardinal signs begin a season; fixed signs occupy its middle; mutable signs precede its transition. Counts are an unweighted description of ten bodies, not a ranking of personal capacities.

Sampling

Unknown or approximate time uses a midpoint and 15-minute samples. Observed sign alternatives do not certify every instant between samples; ASC, houses and aspects are omitted.

Generational

Uranus, Neptune and Pluto move slowly. Their sign and mutual-aspect passages describe a broad shared backdrop; personal contacts and supported houses provide more individual context.

Historical-Time

The engine uses an embedded IANA timezone database for the supplied local date. Historical records, especially before 1970, can be incomplete. City coordinates and current zone assignments are not proof of the exact historical birth location.
